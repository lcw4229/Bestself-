# Grammar Reference — the engine's rule-lookup table

The `spanish-answer-engine` reads this to resolve the correct form for any item. Sections
are ordered by how often the contrast decides a TAMU placement answer (earliest = highest
frequency). Each entry is the **rule** + the **traps** that separate a graded-correct
answer from a plausible-wrong one. Conjugation forms live in `03-verb-reference.md`;
per-item output format lives in `07-answer-formats.md`.

---

## Unit 1 — Preterite vs. Imperfect  ★ highest frequency

**Preterite** = an action viewed as *completed/bounded*: single events, sequences,
sudden reactions, a defined number of times, actions with a start/end.
- Trigger words: *ayer, anoche, de repente, una vez, el lunes pasado, en 2010, entonces*.

**Imperfect** = the *background*: habitual/repeated actions, ongoing description, time,
age, weather, physical/mental/emotional states in progress; "was ~ing".
- Trigger words: *siempre, todos los días, mientras, generalmente, de niño, mientras*.

**Meaning-changing verbs (memorize):**
| Verb | Imperfect | Preterite |
|---|---|---|
| saber | *sabía* = knew (a fact) | *supo* = found out |
| conocer | *conocía* = knew (a person) | *conoció* = met (first time) |
| querer | *quería* = wanted | *quiso* = tried / *no quiso* = refused |
| poder | *podía* = was able (general) | *pudo* = managed / *no pudo* = failed to |
| tener | *tenía* = had | *tuvo* = got/received |

**Traps:** two past actions where one interrupts another → interrupted = imperfect,
interrupter = preterite (*Yo **leía** cuando **sonó** el teléfono*). Irregular preterites
(a whole family): *estuve, tuve, pude, puse, supe, hice, quise, vine, dije, traje,
fui/fue, di, vi*. No accents on these irregular stems.

## Unit 2 — Subjunctive (present + imperfect)  ★ decides the top placement

Mood, not a tense. Used in a **dependent clause** when the main clause imposes a
condition. Mnemonic **WEIRDO**: **W**ish/will, **E**motion, **I**mpersonal
expressions, **R**equests/recommendations, **D**oubt/denial, **O**jalá.

**Trigger families (main clause) → subjunctive in the que-clause:**
- Volition/influence: *querer que, pedir que, insistir en que, recomendar que, es
  necesario que*.
- Emotion: *alegrarse de que, temer que, sentir que, esperar que, es una lástima que*.
- Doubt/denial: *dudar que, no creer que, negar que, no es cierto que, es imposible que*.
  (Note the flip: *creer que* / *es cierto que* → **indicative**; negate them → subjunctive.)
- Impersonal: *es importante/posible/mejor/bueno que* (but *es verdad/evidente que* →
  indicative).
- **Ojalá (que)** → always subjunctive.

**Adjective (relative) clauses:** subjunctive when the antecedent is **unknown,
indefinite, or non-existent**: *Busco un coche que **sea** barato* (any such car) vs.
*Tengo un coche que **es** barato* (a specific one). *No hay nadie que **sepa**…*.

**Adverbial clauses:**
- **Always subjunctive** (the "ESCAPA" conjunctions): *para que, antes (de) que, sin
  que, con tal (de) que, a menos que, en caso de que*.
- **Subjunctive only if the action is future/unrealized** (time conjunctions):
  *cuando, hasta que, en cuanto, tan pronto como, después (de) que, mientras*.
  *Cuando **llegue**, comemos* (hasn't happened) vs. *Cuando **llega**, comemos*
  (habitual) vs. *Cuando **llegó**, comimos* (past → indicative).

**Sequence of tenses:**
- Main verb present/future/command → **present subjunctive**.
- Main verb past (preterite/imperfect) or conditional → **imperfect subjunctive**
  (*-ra* endings from the 3rd-person-plural preterite stem: *hablaran, comieran,
  fueran, tuvieran, pudieran, hicieran*).

**Present-subjunctive irregular yo-based stems:** *tenga, ponga, salga, diga, haga,
oiga, venga, conozca*; fully irregular: *dé, esté, sea, vaya, sepa, haya*.

## Unit 3 — Ser vs. Estar

**Ser** — permanent/defining: identity, origin/nationality, profession, time & date,
material, possession, where an **event** takes place (*La fiesta **es** en mi casa*),
impersonal generalizations.
**Estar** — condition/location: physical location of things/people, health, emotions,
progressive (*estar* + gerund), result of a change (*está roto*).

**Meaning shifts:** *ser aburrido* (boring) vs. *estar aburrido* (bored); *ser listo*
(clever) vs. *estar listo* (ready); *ser rico* (rich) vs. *estar rico* (tastes good);
*ser verde* (green) vs. *estar verde* (unripe); *ser malo* (bad person) vs. *estar malo*
(sick).

## Unit 4 — Por vs. Para

**Para → the arrow points at a goal/endpoint:** destination (*salgo para Madrid*),
purpose/in-order-to (*para aprender*), recipient (*para ti*), deadline (*para el lunes*),
opinion (*para mí*), comparison/"considering" (*para un niño, lee bien*), employment
(*trabajo para…*).

**Por → the cause/means/exchange:** reason/motive (*lo hago por amor*), duration
(*por dos horas*), movement through/along (*por el parque*), exchange/price
(*gracias por…, lo compré por $10*), means (*por teléfono*), "per" (*por hora*),
passive agent (*escrito por*), substitution (*lo hago por ti* = in your place).

**Fixed phrases to memorize:** *por eso, por supuesto, por favor, por fin, por lo menos,
por ejemplo, por cierto, por primera vez, para siempre, para colmo, no es para tanto*.

## Unit 5 — Object Pronouns & Commands

**Direct (DO):** me, te, lo/la, nos, os, los/las. **Indirect (IO):** me, te, **le**,
nos, os, **les**. Double object → **IO before DO**; **le/les → se** before
lo/la/los/las (*Se lo di*). Clarify with *a él/a ellos* when ambiguous.

**Placement:** before a conjugated verb (*Lo veo*); attached to an infinitive or gerund
(*voy a **hacerlo** / **haciéndolo***); attached to affirmative commands, detached and
before negative commands (*¡**Dímelo**! / ¡No me lo digas!*). Attachment usually needs a
**written accent** to keep stress (*dá**me**lo, escri**bién**dola*).

**Commands:**
- Formal Ud./Uds. and negative-tú: use the **present-subjunctive** form (*hable, no
  hables, coman, no comas*).
- Affirmative tú: 3rd-person present, plus **irregulars**: *di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé,
  ten, ven*.
- Nosotros ("let's"): present subjunctive (*hagamos*) or *vamos a* + infinitive.

## Unit 6 — Compound & Future/Conditional (mid frequency)

- **Present perfect:** *he/has/ha/hemos/han* + past participle. **Pluperfect:**
  *había* + participle. Irregular participles: *escrito, dicho, hecho, puesto, visto,
  roto, vuelto, abierto, muerto, cubierto, resuelto*.
- **Future & conditional** share irregular stems: *dir-, har-, pondr-, tendr-, saldr-,
  vendr-, podr-, sabr-, querr-, habr-, valdr-*. Future endings *-é -ás -á -emos -án*;
  conditional *-ía -ías -ía -íamos -ían*. Future/conditional of probability
  (*¿Qué hora **será**?* = I wonder…).

## Unit 7 — Verbs like *gustar* & pronouns

- **gustar-type:** *gustar, encantar, faltar, quedar, doler, importar, interesar,
  molestar, parecer*. Structure: IO pronoun + verb agreeing with the **thing**
  (*Me **gustan** los libros*; *A ella le **duele** la cabeza*).
- **Relative pronouns:** *que* (default), *quien(es)* (after prepositions, people),
  *lo que* ("what/that which"), *cuyo/-a/-os/-as* (whose, agrees with the thing owned),
  *el/la/los/las cual(es)* (formal, after prepositions).

## Unit 8 — Agreement, comparatives, negatives, misc.

- Noun–adjective–article gender/number agreement; adjectives of nationality; position.
- Comparatives: *más/menos … que*, *tan … como*, *tanto/-a/-os/-as … como*.
  Irregulars: *mejor, peor, mayor, menor*. Superlative: *el más … de*; absolute *-ísimo*.
- Negatives & doubles: *no … nada / nadie / nunca / ningún / tampoco / ni…ni*.
- Interrogatives with accents (*qué, quién, cuál, cuándo, dónde, cómo, cuánto, por qué*).
- **Personal *a*** before a specific human/pet direct object (*Veo **a** María*).
- Saber (facts/how-to) vs. conocer (be familiar with a person/place).

## Unit 9 — Vocabulary breadth (for reading + adaptive items)

Prioritize high-frequency thematic sets: family & daily routine, house/city, food &
restaurant, travel/directions, health/body, work/professions, school, weather/nature,
technology, feelings, time expressions. Learn **false friends** (*embarazada* =
pregnant, *éxito* = success, *asistir* = to attend, *realizar* = to carry out,
*actualmente* = currently, *sensible* = sensitive, *librería* = bookstore) and use
cognate/morphology inference for unknown words in reading passages.

## Unit 10 — Reading & Writing exam skills

**Reading:** read the question first, skim for gist, scan for the targeted detail, use
context for unknown words, reject "true-but-too-narrow" main-idea options, and substitute
options back into the sentence for vocab-in-context.

**Writing/essay:** favor correct over ambitious. Deliberately deploy range you can
control — ≥1 subjunctive, a preterite+imperfect contrast, connectors (*sin embargo, por
lo tanto, aunque, ya que, además, por otro lado*), varied vocabulary. Perfect agreement
and accents. Structure: thesis → 2 supported points → conclusion. Proofread for
ser/estar, por/para, and verb endings before submitting.
